

# The Healing Power of Performing Arts — Academic Website

A minimal, scroll-based single-page website with a calm, thoughtful aesthetic.

## Design
- **Colors**: Warm beige/cream background (`#FAF8F5`), soft muted accents (dusty rose, warm grey), dark charcoal text
- **Typography**: Clean serif for headings (Georgia/system serif), sans-serif for body text
- **Layout**: Full-width sections with generous whitespace, alternating subtle background tones
- **Visuals**: Minimal decorative elements — thin divider lines, small theatre mask or spotlight icons using Lucide icons, subtle quote styling

## Sections (single scrolling page)

1. **Hero** — Large title "The Healing Power of Performing Arts" with subtitle, centered on a soft cream background with a subtle theatre masks icon
2. **Performing Arts as Emotional Expression** — Content about acting, safe spaces, stress reduction with a clean card or text layout
3. **Performing Arts as an Escape** — Two-column layout: "For Performers" and "For Audiences"
4. **Community and Human Connection** — Key points about collaboration, shared experiences, belonging
5. **Impact on Mental Well-being** — Five benefits displayed as minimal cards/list (confidence, emotional awareness, stress relief, purpose, empathy)
6. **Conclusion** — Summary paragraph with a warm closing statement
7. **Credits Footer** — Names, roll numbers, and course name in a clean footer

## Navigation
- Simple sticky top nav with section links for easy scrolling
- Smooth scroll behavior

All content will be hardcoded — no backend needed.
